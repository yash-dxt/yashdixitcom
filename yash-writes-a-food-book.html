Flavours of India - A Journey Through Food & Cities

By Yash Dixit

Foreword

I’ve travelled a lot through India, and yet it seems like I’ve covered so little. I still have to travel more and more. I still have to go to places where I’ve already been, not just once again, but multiple times.

I’m not completely sure if I love India or not, but I am sure that it has everything a traveller, or in my case, a gastronome needs.

Every hundred miles, in any direction, will give you a different language, a different attire, and most importantly, a different cuisine.

It’s amazing how if you taste a good pyaaz kachori in Jodhpur, you won’t find the same taste in Ajmer (which is about two hundred kilometers away), but you will find a completely new taste in kachori. Keep going a few miles in any direction and you’ll get a kachori or another dish with a completely different taste, but I’m sure it will be good.

India is an adventure; this I know for sure.

It’s not for the faint-hearted. Things aren’t safe, things aren’t comfortable, and it’s very overwhelming. You might meet amazing people, and you will meet scammers. But in the end, you’ll get through all those (maybe, as I said, it’s an adventure) and have an amazing meal, and I’m sure you’ll love the meal.

I’m going to write about my experiences in a very random way, very unstructured mostly. Let’s see how this goes. Dive in.
